These settings are local to this Chrome profile. Room messages are encrypted before they reach the relay.

Your first watch together

  1. Start the relay locally, or enter your shared secure relay below.
  2. Open a title and start playback on Netflix, Prime Video, or YouTube.
  3. Choose Create room in Cwtch, then share the private invite.
  4. Your person installs Cwtch and opens the invite in Chrome.

Both people need access to the same title. A local relay works only on this computer; two computers need a shared wss:// relay.

Connection

Use ws://localhost:8787/ws while developing. Public HTTPS use requires a TLS-enabled wss:// address.

Your side of the room

What Cwtch does not do

No video relay. The extension never captures, decrypts, uploads, or rebroadcasts streaming content.

No readable room traffic. Chat, moments, reactions, and playback events use the secret inside the invite link.

No account database. Your display name stays on this device. Room keys and tab identities are kept for this browser session.

Your data

Saved conversation history is optional. Clear it from this device at any time. This does not delete your person’s copy.
